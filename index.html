FAQ

Questions, unhurried.

What if I actually need to be somewhere?

Set your finish line a little earlier than you need to leave. Dawdle never makes you late; it just doesn't make you panic.

Does Dawdle work if I'm a heavy sleeper?

Yes. Heavy sleepers can switch on "Persistent Politeness," which gets gradually, kindly, undeniably louder.

Is this a real product?

Not yet! Dawdle is a fictional product built as a landing-page design exercise. The email form below doesn't store anything.

Why are the plans called Eventually, Whenever and Someday?

Because naming them Basic, Pro and Enterprise felt too much like a Monday.
